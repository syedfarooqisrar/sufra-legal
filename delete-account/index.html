Delete your Sufra account

You can delete your Sufra account and the data linked to it in either of the two ways below.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open Sufra and go to the Home tab.
  2. Tap the settings icon (top right).
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Option 2: By email

If you can no longer open the app, email YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL from the email address you registered with, using the subject Delete my Sufra account. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile, settings and family information (names, ages, diet and allergy details)
  • Your favorites and history
  • Your creator profile, posts and uploaded photos
  • Your follows, likes, reports and blocks

What may remain

Shared, non-personal content that is not linked to any account, such as cached recipe text, is not deleted.
